# After an argument: debrief sheet

*Each partner fills this in on their own, at least a few hours after the argument, when calm. Bring it to the next session or swap sheets only if you both agree.*

**Date of the argument:** ____ **How long ago:** ____

1. **What happened**, as a camera would record it (no "he always", no motives): ____
2. **What I told myself** in the moment about what it meant: ____
3. **What I felt**, and how strong (0-10): ____
4. **What I did** (words, tone, leaving, going quiet): ____
5. **What else could be true** about what my partner did or meant? Two other possibilities: ____
6. **What I needed** in that moment and didn't ask for: ____
7. **My part:** one thing I'd do differently: ____
8. **One sentence I could say to start repairing:** ____

> If the argument involved being afraid, being hurt, threats or someone being stopped from leaving: don't use this sheet. Talk to your practitioner alone, or contact emergency services (112 / 911) or a helpline (findahelpline.com).

# Starting a hard conversation gently

*Practice sheet. The first sentence often decides how a conversation goes. A gentle start describes the situation and your feeling, and asks for something concrete, without a verdict about the other person.*

**Formula:** I feel ____ about ____ (the situation, not the person). I need / would like ____.

| Harsh start | Gentle start |
|---|---|
| "You never help with anything." | "I'm exhausted from doing the evenings alone this week. Could you take bath and bedtime on Tuesday and Thursday?" |
| "You care more about your phone than me." | "I miss talking at dinner. Could we keep phones off the table this week?" |
| "Your mother is impossible." | "I felt hurt by the comment about my job on Sunday. Can we talk about how to handle visits?" |

*Examples are invented.*

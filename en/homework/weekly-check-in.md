# Weekly couple check-in (20 minutes)

*Same time each week, phones away, no solving big problems in this slot.*

1. **Appreciation (3 min):** each says one specific thing the other did this week that they appreciated. "Thank you for..." only, no "but".
2. **What went well between us (3 min):** one moment each.
3. **Logistics (5 min):** calendar, money, kids, chores for the coming week. Who owns what.
4. **One small issue (7 min):** each may raise one small issue, using a gentle start (see [gentle-start](gentle-start.md)). The listener summarises before replying. Bigger issues get a separate time or go to the next session.
5. **One wish for the week (2 min):** "Next week I'd love it if we..."

Optional before step 1: both fill in the 2-minute [Couple pulse](https://pairclear.github.io/relationship-questionnaires/?q=couple-pulse&utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=couples-counseling-kit) and compare.

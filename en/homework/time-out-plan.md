# Time-out plan for heated arguments

*Agree on this together on a calm day, and sign it. A time-out is a pause to calm down, not a way to win or to punish.*

- **Our signal for a pause** (word or gesture): ____
- **Who can call it:** either of us, any time.
- **What we say:** "I'm getting too heated. I'm taking ____ minutes and I'll come back."
- **How long:** at least 20 minutes, no more than ____ hours.
- **During the pause I will** (calm down, not rehearse the argument): walk / shower / breathe / music / ____
- **I won't:** text the argument, drink, drive angry, talk to others to recruit them.
- **When we come back,** the person who called the pause starts by saying it's okay to continue, or proposes a new time.
- **If one of us blocks the other from leaving, threatens, breaks things or hurts anyone:** the conversation ends and we tell our practitioner. If anyone is in danger: 112 / 911.

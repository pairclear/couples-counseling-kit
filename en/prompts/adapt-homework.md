# Prompt: adapt a homework sheet

Use to adapt one of the [homework sheets](../homework/) for a particular couple's situation and reading level, without client details.

```text
Adapt the homework sheet below for a couple. Do not add new exercises; keep its purpose.

Context (no names, no identifying details): [for example "both work night shifts, two teenagers, arguments are mostly about money, English is their second language"]
Make it: [shorter / simpler language / more examples / for one partner only]
Keep: the safety note, the structure, the fill-in blanks.
Do not: diagnose, assign blame to either partner, or add claims about effectiveness.

Sheet:
[paste the sheet]
```

# Prompt: check a text for jargon and blame

Run your handout, email or homework through this before sending.

```text
Review the text below for a couple with no psychology background. List, with the exact phrase:
1. Jargon or terms that need explaining.
2. Phrases that could sound like blame toward either partner, or like a diagnosis or label.
3. Claims that sound stronger than the evidence ("proven", "always works").
4. Anything that tells them to leave or stay.
5. Whether a safety line (violence, threats, fear of a partner: emergency services, findahelpline.com) is present where it matters.
Then suggest a plainer rewrite of the worst three sentences. Don't rewrite the whole text.

Text:
[paste]
```

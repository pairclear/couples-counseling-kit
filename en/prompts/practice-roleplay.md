# Prompt: practise a session with a simulated couple (for training)

For trainees and practitioners who want to rehearse, for example, slowing down an escalation or setting a time-out. The couple is invented. It is practice, not supervision: discuss what you learn with your supervisor.

```text
Role-play an invented couple in a counselling session so I can practise. I am the practitioner.

Couple: [for example "together 9 years, one child, argue about time with friends vs family; one pursues, the other withdraws"]
Skill I want to practise: [for example "interrupting escalation and slowing the pace"]
Difficulty: [easy / medium / hard]

Rules:
- Play both partners, labelled A and B. Keep replies short and realistic, including interruptions.
- Do not include violence, abuse or suicide unless I ask for a safety-screening practice; if I do, make the signs subtle and realistic, and stay respectful.
- When I type "pause", step out of role and give me brief feedback: one thing that worked, one thing to try, in two or three sentences.
- When I type "end", give a short summary of the moments where the couple softened or escalated, and why.
```

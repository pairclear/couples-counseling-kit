# Prompt: structure your own session notes (de-identified)

Some practitioners use AI to turn rough notes into a structured draft. **Before you do, check your professional code, your contract with clients and data-protection law (in the EU, GDPR: health and sex-life data are special-category data).** Prefer tools with a data-processing agreement and no training on your data. If in doubt, don't.

Before pasting anything, remove: names (use A and B), ages, places, employers, dates, unusual events or details that could identify someone. Read the result critically: AI can add things that weren't said.

```text
Turn these de-identified rough notes into the structure below. Use only what is in the notes. Do not add interpretations, diagnoses or risk judgements; if something is unclear, write "[unclear]". Keep observations and the practitioner's hypotheses in separate sections.

Structure:
- Since last session
- Focus of this session
- Observations (what was said and done)
- Practitioner's hypotheses (only if written as hypotheses in the notes)
- Interventions
- Homework
- Plan for next session
- Risk: copy exactly what the notes say, or write "not recorded"

Notes:
[paste de-identified notes]
```

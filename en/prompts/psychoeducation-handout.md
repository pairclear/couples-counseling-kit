# Prompt: a psychoeducation handout for a couple

Use to draft a one-page handout on a topic (for example the pursue-withdraw cycle, how arguments escalate, the mental load, repair after a fight). You review and edit it before giving it to anyone. **Do not include client details.**

```text
You are helping a couples practitioner draft a one-page handout for clients.

Topic: [TOPIC]
Audience: [for example "a couple in their 30s with small children, no psychology background"]
Language: [English]
Length: about 350 words.

Write it like this:
- Title as a plain question the couple might ask.
- "What it is": 3-4 sentences in plain words, no jargon (if a term is needed, explain it once).
- "How it might look at home": 2 short invented examples, marked as invented.
- "What tends to help": 3-5 concrete things a couple can try this week.
- "When to get more help": one short paragraph, including that violence, threats or fear of a partner need a different kind of help (emergency 112/911, findahelpline.com).
- "Where this comes from": name the research tradition or author in one sentence, and say if evidence is limited. If you are not sure of a source, say "check the source" instead of inventing one.

Rules: no diagnoses or labels for either partner; no blame; don't tell anyone to leave or stay; no claims like "proven to save marriages"; never invent citations, statistics or quotes.
```

**Checklist before handing it out:** facts and sources checked by you; examples don't resemble any real client; reading level fits the couple; safety line kept.

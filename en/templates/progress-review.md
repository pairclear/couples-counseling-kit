## Ask each partner
- What is different at home since we started, even slightly?
- What hasn't moved that you hoped would?
- What has been most useful in sessions? Least useful?
- Do you want to continue, change focus, pause or end?

Optional: both fill in the [Couple pulse](https://pairclear.github.io/relationship-questionnaires/?q=couple-pulse&utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=couples-counseling-kit) questionnaire and compare with the first time. It is not validated; use it as a conversation starter, not an outcome measure.

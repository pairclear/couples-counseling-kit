# Couple intake: first session

*A template for psychologists, counsellors and coaches working with couples. Fill it in your own records system, not in an AI chat. Adapt it to your training, your jurisdiction and your professional code.*

## 1. Before joint work: individual safety check

Ask each partner **separately** (a short individual call or the first 10 minutes alone), not in front of the other:

- Have you ever felt afraid of your partner?
- Has your partner ever pushed, hit, choked, threatened or forced you?
- Does your partner control your money, phone, movements or who you see?
- Have you ever been afraid for your children's safety?

> If there is current violence, coercive control or fear, joint sessions can increase risk. Follow your safety protocol, offer individual support and specialist services, and do not share one partner's disclosure with the other.
